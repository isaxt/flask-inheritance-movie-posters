THE SCOOP

Created by 2 programmers who are avid movie fanatics and were tired of the lack of centralized movie information, THE SCOOP is an open source project dedicated to providing you with all things movies — stats, trends, ticket pricing, ratings, etc.

No longer are the days of switching between local movie sites, AMC/REGAL, IMDB, and Rotten Tomatos to get information about a movie that you wanted to watch, THE SCOOP does it all for you.

Simple, intuitive, and easy to navigate, explore the site now to get started.
